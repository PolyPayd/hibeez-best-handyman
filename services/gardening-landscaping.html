About This Trade

How we approach gardening & landscaping.

A garden that's been left for a season or two can feel like a bigger job than it actually is. We clear overgrown areas, remove waste and old materials, and get the space back to a usable base - whether that's for turfing, a new shed, or just having somewhere to sit again.

The shed installation in our gallery is a typical job: an overgrown, cluttered base cleared back to the existing slabs, then a new storage shed assembled and installed square and secure. We handle the clearance and the build as one job, so there's no gap between 'cleared' and 'finished'.

Common Questions

Gardening & Landscaping FAQs

Do you remove garden waste after clearance?

Yes - waste and green refuse removal is part of the clearance service, so you're not left with a pile to deal with yourself.

Can you assemble a shed I've already bought?

Yes, we regularly assemble flat-pack and panel sheds, including preparing or levelling the base first if needed.

Do you lay artificial grass?

Yes, including sub-base preparation and edging for a level, long-lasting result.
