About This Trade

How we approach plumbing.

A dripping tap or running toilet wastes water and money every single day it's left. We fix the common plumbing problems that come up in every home - leaks, worn washers, faulty valves, blocked or slow-draining fixtures - and fit new sinks, taps and toilets cleanly with proper sealing so there's nothing to come back to.

Plumbing sits alongside tiling and carpentry in a lot of our jobs - a bathroom refit, a new kitchen sink, a utility room plumbed in for a washing machine. Having one team handle the plumbing and the finishing work means fewer trades to coordinate and a tidier result.

Common Questions

Plumbing FAQs

Do you handle emergency leaks?

Message or call us and we'll get to you as fast as we can - same-day service where possible across Gillingham and the wider Medway area.

Can you fit a new bathroom suite?

Yes, including toilets, basins, taps and showers, with sealant and finishing done properly so there's no seepage down the line.

Do you do boiler or gas work?

No - boiler and gas work needs a Gas Safe registered engineer. We handle the general plumbing: taps, toilets, leaks, sinks and fixture installs.
