About This Trade

How we approach electrical.

Whether it's a statement chandelier in a hallway or a bank of downlights above a new media wall, electrical work needs to be done properly - hidden faults cause real problems down the line. We fit lighting, sockets and switches cleanly, safely, and with the wiring routed and secured the way it should be.

We've fitted everything from single-bulb pendant swaps to multi-tier crystal chandeliers on plastered ceilings across Gillingham and Medway. If it's a bigger rewire or consumer unit job needing a registered electrician sign-off, we'll tell you upfront and can point you to who to call - we're not going to take on work outside what's safe for us to do.

Common Questions

Electrical FAQs

Can you fit a chandelier or heavy light fixture?

Yes - we regularly fit multi-tier chandeliers and pendant fixtures, including on plaster ceilings where extra bracing or a proper ceiling rose fixing is needed.

Do you do full house rewires?

For smaller electrical jobs - lighting, sockets, switches, fixtures - we're your call. Full rewires and consumer unit changes need a registered electrician, and we'll always be upfront if a job needs that instead.

Do you cover outdoor and garden electrics?

Yes, including outdoor lighting circuits and fixtures, fitted with proper outdoor-rated cabling and connections.
