About This Trade

How we approach tiling & flooring.

Tiling and flooring are unforgiving trades - an uneven subfloor or a badly cut tile shows immediately and permanently. We prep the surface properly first, so the finished floor is level and the tile lines run true from the first row to the last.

We fit everything from a full bathroom tiling job with proper waterproof tanking, to laminate or vinyl flooring through a living room or hallway. If a floor's going in under new carpentry work - a media wall or built-in unit - we sequence it so the flooring runs cleanly under and around it.

Common Questions

Tiling & Flooring FAQs

Do you tile wet rooms and showers?

Yes, including proper waterproof tanking before tiling to prevent leaks and damp behind the tile line.

What flooring types can you fit?

Laminate, engineered wood, vinyl and LVT flooring, plus underlay and subfloor prep to make sure it sits level and wears well.

Can you match new tiling to existing tiles?

Where possible we'll source a close match, though exact tile matches depend on what's still available from the original range.
