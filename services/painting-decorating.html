About This Trade

How we approach painting & decorating.

Most paint jobs live or die on the prep, not the paint. We fill, sand and mask properly before a brush or roller goes anywhere near a wall, which is why the finish holds up - no bleed under tape, no patchy second coats, no touch-ups needed a month later.

We paint everything from a single feature wall to a full interior repaint, and we're just as comfortable finishing decorative wall panelling as we are painting a plain ceiling. If carpentry work like panelling or a media wall is part of the job, we can handle the paint finish as part of the same visit.

Common Questions

Painting & Decorating FAQs

Do you match existing paint colours?

Yes, we can colour-match an existing wall or work from a specific paint reference or Farrow & Ball / Dulux code you provide.

Can you paint newly installed wall panelling?

Yes - we regularly paint panelling and feature walls we've just built, so the carpentry and the finish are handled by the same team.

Do you do exterior painting in winter?

Exterior painting depends on weather - we'll advise honestly if conditions aren't right for a durable finish and help you plan for a suitable window.
