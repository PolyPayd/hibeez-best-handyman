About This Trade

How we approach plastering & drywall.

Plastering is the trade nobody notices when it's done well - and impossible to hide when it isn't. We patch small cracks and holes, skim full walls and ceilings, and build stud walls with boarding ready for finishing, all with a flat, even surface that paint or tile sits properly on.

It's often the first step in a bigger job: a new stud wall to divide a room, a full skim after removing old artex, or making good after other work has damaged a wall. We plan it as part of the wider job so the timeline and drying times don't hold up whatever comes next.

Common Questions

Plastering & Drywall FAQs

How long does plaster need to dry before painting?

Fresh skim typically needs 3–5 days to dry depending on room conditions before it's ready for mist-coat painting. We'll advise the right timeline for your job.

Can you patch a small area without redoing the whole wall?

Yes - for small cracks or holes we can patch-repair and blend into the existing surface rather than skimming the full wall.

Do you build stud walls for new rooms?

Yes, including timber stud framing, plasterboard and finishing ready for paint or tiling.
