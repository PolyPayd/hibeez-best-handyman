About This Trade

How we approach carpentry & joinery.

This is where the trade side of the business shows the most - media walls with recessed lighting and built-in storage, alcove shelving fitted flush to an awkward chimney breast, wall panelling that turns a plain room into a feature. It's proper joinery, not just flat-pack assembly.

We measure, template and build for the actual room, not a standard size. The before-and-after fireplace media wall build in our gallery is a real example: a dated fireplace and blank alcoves became a fully wired, lit entertainment wall with hidden cable management - carpentry and electrical work planned together from the start.

Common Questions

Carpentry & Joinery FAQs

Can you design a media wall around an existing fireplace?

Yes - we build around existing chimney breasts, fireplaces and stoves, and can incorporate an electric fire, lighting and cable management into the design.

Do you supply the timber and materials?

We can supply materials as part of the quote, or work with materials you've already bought - whichever works better for your budget.

How long does a media wall build typically take?

Most media wall or alcove shelving builds take 2–5 days depending on complexity, lighting and finishing requirements. We'll give you a clear timeline in the quote.
