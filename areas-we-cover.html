We operate out of Gillingham in the Medway towns, which puts most of Kent and parts of South East London within a comfortable drive. As a rough guide:

  • Core area (under 20 min): Gillingham, Chatham, Rochester, Rainham, Strood - fastest response, most flexible scheduling.

Common Questions

Coverage FAQs

Do you charge extra for jobs outside Gillingham?

For our core Medway area there's no travel surcharge. For jobs further out in Kent, any travel time is factored transparently into the quote - never a surprise on the day.

What if I'm just outside your listed areas?

Message us your postcode. Our coverage is flexible around a roughly one-hour drive from Gillingham, and we'll always tell you honestly if a job's too far out rather than taking it on and running late.

Do you take on commercial jobs as well as residential?

Yes - we work with both homeowners and small businesses across our coverage area.
